vanitas-veritas/davos_2027

proposal · 29.09.2026 · updated 15.10.2026

veri+as

Working notes for a piece by Kat Titterton, in conversation with Olive Allen, proposed for Davos in January 2027. It starts from the sink in Vanitas / Veritas and asks what happens when the water is allowed to move, and when nothing that goes down the drain is allowed to leave.

The seed of 29 September, updated. What is not built is marked in progress or proposed. The piece: veri+as iii. The context: the black paper.

00  thesis

  • A new biology. New forms of life; the uncanny in-between, of life and death and of species; emergence; softness.
  • you can't wash your hands of it. A sink you cannot wash your hands in. Pictures dreamed by AI drain away and come back from the tap, copies of copies. Generative models now train on their own output; so do markets; so do feeds.
  • sink == sync. Two clocks on one beam fall into step (Huygens); a crowd of clocks does once the coupling passes a threshold (Kuramoto). The glass is a crowd of clocks. When enough people hold still at once, the rhythm breaks and the title turns from vanitas to veritas.
  • we. The answer to the riddle, and the credit line: macrofluidic × we.
  • birth another. Every black hole births a new universe, in Lee Smolin's fecund universes. At the centre of every youniverse is a black hole.
a new biologywe need a new biology. The sink is a closed loop: what goes down the drain comes back out of the tap. What keeps coming back grows new forms of life, each from two of the loop's pictures: a face with the skull showing through, a skull in flower, bubbles becoming cells. Between life and death, between species; emergent, soft. merge.
resistanceresistance is futile. Scrub the glass and it only spreads; the hand comes away gold. you can't wash your hands of it. Wipe the culture on the rim and it divides and grows back. it's all ok.
okit shall be ok. Hold still and the glass clears. Seven stars hide under it; each one found grows a new form of life and writes a line of the program. beautiful even. great even.
the spotlightyou can't hide. At the centre is a black hole, and it is an eye. If you let it, it takes one frame from your camera, kept on your device and never sent. You become the centre of your youniverse, and most of what you grow after that carries your face, the bone of the dream showing through. the spotlight.
the skyEvery find lights a star. Seven make a youniverse, named in Latin as code (spuma_exspectans_concubii: the foam that waits, at first sleep), with the culture your hands pushed. The end reads you back: a line of poem over a line of code that counts what you did. Two letters finish the credit line and open the black paper.

01  the piece

open it full size ->  ·  earlier: i, ii

i (29 September) and ii (5 October) are kept as they were. One clock carries into iii: it runs from 29 September to the first day of Davos, and each day a little less is poured and the clocks in the glass are coupled tighter (?day=N looks ahead).

A basin seen from above, under fluted glass, ringed by a living culture. It is born from nothing: a flash, a ring of gold, the sphere, the culture growing out of the rim, the title arriving as v[an]i+as and turning once. Nothing explains itself: a star glints, a tap ripples, and the first hold finds its place under the hand. Seven stars hide under the glass. Each grows a new form of life from two of the loop's 32 pictures, named like a species (Calvaria florens; Ipse, the self, once it has seen you), and adds a line of poem over a line of code. How you play pushes the culture, so each youniverse ends with its own. What drains comes back from the tap ten seconds later, copies of copies. The pulse is 38 bpm, a freediver's heart on a breath-hold.

01b  built, in progress, proposed

built

  • The program. A primer, then a poem, written as Python: a human comment over the machine's code (03).
  • Two poems. The hint poem: one line per find, written from what grew (its join, its two pictures, whether your face is in it, its place in the seven). Kat's seven lines appear only now and then, later finds lean toward we, and return youniverse(me + you) shows from the start, so anyone who reads code can solve the riddle at once. The reading, at the end: pairs of a kind, slightly obscure line of poem over a line of code that is a literal count of the play (while held < 14.3: stay()). On some days, one line is the same for everyone who played that day. A light play reads short, a deep one long, from three pairs to ten. What chooses the lines stays hidden.
  • Holding together. Every hold, anywhere, is a ring in everyone's basin, carried over public Nostr relays: no server, no accounts. Every day at noon, Davos time, everyone holds. The coupling tightens toward January: in October one long breath can turn the title alone; by January it takes more than one.
  • The study. Each play leaves one anonymous record on public Nostr relays (NIP-78): counts of the play, the shape of its stars, the forms of life by name, the culture and, at the end, the youniverse's name and code. No picture, no face. ?nostudy or Global Privacy Control keeps a play out; there is no opt-out button, by design.
  • Keeping and sending. A youniverse travels as a 52-character link, never a picture. It goes out by symbol (X, Bluesky, Threads, LinkedIn), or with its card through the phone's own share sheet, or saved, or copied; the raw link is never shown. Every youniverse grown stays on the device. birth another starts a new one.

in progress

  • The reading, redrawn: left-aligned like code and poems, big to small, the name once, symbols.
  • The macroverse in 3D: youniverses placed by how alike they were played, with clusters and filaments forming after the fact, turning slightly. Each youniverse is a flat constellation that folds into its 3D sky when opened, never both at once, with its culture shown. home frames your own; the weverse lights you and your friends.

proposed

  • The black hole grows. The drain grows into a black hole as you find stars, of a kind set by how you played: still, or spinning; rarer, a quasar, a torn star, two merging when you held with someone, a pulsar or a supernova; very rarely, a white hole.
  • birth another, as rebirth. The old youniverse falls into its black hole and a new one is born from it, inheriting its culture with a small mutation. Lineages in the macroverse.
  • Faces in the macroverse, opt-in only. Only the dreamed image, never the raw frame. A separate consent step: 18 or over, only you in the frame, a separate yes for the Davos projection, a removal code. Kept privately in the EU, every image approved by Kat before it appears anywhere, and an expiry date. No promise that a face cannot be recognised. No face landmarks or face-shape detection. Reviewed by a Swiss or EU data-protection lawyer before Davos.
  • The sink. One real sink in Davos, running the same page (04b).

02  what style makes sense

The lenticular. The ridged glass in the singulari+i image and the ridged plastic on a 2000s holo trading card are the same optical object. One is a lens you look through; the other is a collectible you tilt. A lenticular shows two pictures from one surface depending on where you stand, which is already the whole premise of vanitas_veritas.

September's rules: signs are drawn with one stroke weight, like pictograms on a 2003 device. Fluid is cel-shaded with an inked contour and one hard highlight, so it reads as cartoon, then given real grain and a soft second highlight so it does not quite. No periodic waves. Colour arrives only in a pour or in the foil.

Third pass, after "weird, mysterious, apocalyptic, shamanistic": the sink becomes a scrying basin. Same lens, same signs, but staged as a rite. The disc is a black sun with a fibrous corona that brightens on the beat. Ash rises. Ember and ice are the only colours, one pour at a time. Fog crosses the rim and the glass is scratched. Space is the other material: the disc is cropped by the edge of the screen and the words sit small in what is left.

Where it went. The black sun became a black hole at the centre, an eye that lights as the sky fills. Ash, fog and the ring of cuts were dropped. Colour now also lives in the stars and in the culture's threads.

Kat is in conversation with Olive Allen about the work; Olive's part in it is not yet set. What I know of Olive's work is public and mostly 2019 to 2022 (Pokémon cards, Furbies, UnBearables, drops, the passport burn). If her current work has moved, this table should move with it.

03  the program

# what drains away comes back
sink = drain(sink)
# we have not seen you yet
you = None
# every clock keeps one time
sink == sync
# a youniverse, grown from you
def youniverse(you):
  # what grows here, and how fast
  culture = grow(f=.0310, k=.0600)
  # the intention: be still, and look
  while held(0.0): seek(warm=.00)
  # and again, together
  return youniverse(me + you)

04  six ways in

adouble vanity

A double vanity is a bathroom unit with two sinks. Proposed: two basins, one loop. What drains from one pours from the other's tap, and back, side by side or in two cities. The holds already travel between basins over public relays; a second sink would join them.

Davos reading: two parties who cannot wash their hands without the other receiving it.

bthe cards

Proposed: lenticular cards, vanitas at one tilt and veritas at the other. Built: any form of life, and every youniverse, can be kept as a card and sent.

In September, eight signs issued as a set. Eight became seven stars and the eye.

cliquidity pool

The basin taken literally. Each visitor adds one drop of pigment. It circulates, dilutes, and never leaves. By Friday the pool is the colour of everyone who came. The colour on the last day is the work.

Davos reading: liquidity, trickle-down, and who gets to wash their hands.

dnull address

Olive's Post-death or The Null Address (2021) takes its title from the address tokens are sent to when they are burned. A drain makes the same promise. Here the promise is broken: whatever is burned into the drain comes back out of the tap.

Mechanic: burn to pour.

ehold

Built, in another form: every hold, anywhere, is a ring in everyone's basin. When enough hold at once the rhythm breaks and the title turns from vanitas to veritas. Two handles on one sink, too far apart for one person, is still proposed.

Davos sits at about 1,560 m. Breath is already short.

fsnowmelt

No screen. Real water, a small pump, a fluted acrylic disc over a lit basin, pigment dosed on the heartbeat. The water is snow collected outside the venue on day one and returned on day five.

Hardest to build, easiest to explain.

In September the recommendation was a + b. Where it went: e is built, as the shared hold. The double vanity (a) is proposed for after Davos. The cards (b) are proposed. c, d and f stay ideas.

05  build notes

What the page is, technically. One HTML page, no framework, no server of its own, no accounts. WebGL2. One small library, nostr-tools, carries the shared hold and the study over public relays.

  • Basin: a stable-fluids solver (semi-Lagrangian advection, 36 pressure passes, 24 on phones) on a 256² grid with a source at the tap and a sink at the drain, a swirl that reverses each still, and a 1280² dye field (768² on phones). The dye is the picture: it is fed from the current still and carried by the flow.
  • The return: what is going down the drain is remembered for ten seconds and poured back at the tap, dimmer each pass: copies of copies.
  • Glass: 72 flutes per basin unit. The glass is 2,048 coupled clocks (Kuramoto). In step, bands of light run down the flutes and sink == sync is True. A hold takes tiles out of step, and when enough hold at once the rhythm breaks, the basin clears and the title turns. Each day the coupling is a little stronger.
  • Stills: the 32 anchors the loop was dreamed through (Kat's eight pictures, lightly dreamed, and three dreamed between each pair), 1024² WebP, inlined by build.py. The page walks among them at random. Each find grows a named form of life from two of them, joined as haunt (the living and the dead), graft (across species), culture (grown from the culture's cells) or merge (two as one), in amounts set by how the hand arrived.
  • Culture: Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion on a 4096×256 polar field, drawn as contour threads with glints. It starts at f .031, k .060, where stripes, spots and holes meet. How you play pushes the two numbers: stillness toward a net, moving toward loops, scrubbing toward foam, wiping toward dividing cells, holding with others toward a lattice. Its threads turn red when underfed, gold in the middle, white when fed. Each youniverse ends with its own culture.
  • The face: on a click and the browser's own permission, the eye takes one frame and the camera stops. The frame is graded like the stills and blended into the forms of life inside a fixed oval, with no face detection, at the centre of your youniverse. It stays in the page, on the device: never sent, never stored, gone when the page closes. A card carries it only if you send the card.
  • The youniverse: seven finds make one. A 52-character link (?sky=) carries its stars and each form of life's recipe, never a picture. Whoever opens it finds it under theirs in gold and can grow its forms of life again, with their own face where yours was.
  • Ring of cuts (i only): 240 cuts, long for 1 and short for 0, spelling sink=[sync]*n;n->inf;sink=sync.
  • Finish: a soft-focus pass (quarter-resolution blur, lighter inside the basin), desaturation to 58%, one warm tint, grain. Colours: ink, bone, gold, ice; red at the black hole's edge, in the farthest stars and in a starved culture.
  • Render cost (measured on i): 3 to 9 ms a frame on an M-series laptop at 1.5×2K. Phones get a lighter basin, and without WebGL the page shows one still and the words. Press r to record a 15-second clip.

Toward the installation.

  • The sink as designed for tiat (not built) seals a flat acrylic disc over the basin screen. Swapping it for a fluted or lenticular acrylic sheet gets the lens look optically, with no shader. The on-screen lens in the web basin is a stand-in for that.
  • Built in the page: a sink mode (?sink=davos), the disc only, with Space as the faucet. Proposed for the sink: the lever moves a magnet past a Hall sensor, and a small USB board types Space. A sink keeps nothing and fills again about 75 seconds after each ending. Worth testing early: a Raspberry Pi's GPU may need the simulation grid reduced.

What repeats. Live data as material. Ecology as the sanctioned subject. AI as a partner, never as a problem. Big, luminous, room-scale. And, once, the opposite: Abramović's bus, small, dark, closed, asking for stillness, which she herself doubted executives would give.

What is missing, which is where we stand. Nothing shown has been about AI's own residue, or about the viewer's part in it. Nothing is allowed to end. The one published criticism of the programme (Right Click Save, 2025) is that art at Davos washes reputations and lets policymakers delegate ethics to artists. A sink you cannot wash your hands in answers that directly.

What to take.

  • Be the shortest ritual in Davos. THE BUS asks twenty minutes. We ask one breath. It fits between sessions, and what you grew leaves in the pocket, as a link.
  • Put it where they wash their hands. The bus was parked outside the Congress Centre; JR pasted on facades. The sink belongs in the room people enter alone. Art where the policy briefs cannot go.
  • Live data, but ours is people holding still. Forestate runs on deforestation; Living Archive on weather. Every hold, anywhere, reaches every basin, and one clock runs to the first day of the meeting.
  • Faces: our own, not borrowed. Anadol used the Yawanawá's drawings; JR used strangers' faces. The faces melting in our basin are the artist's. The viewer's, only if they allow it, is grown into what they make and stays on their device; in the macroverse only with a separate yes (proposed). No one is witnessed on someone else's behalf.
  • Duration as material. Sunde stood in the sea for a tide. Every day at noon, Davos time, everyone holds: on the web now, and at the sink in January (proposed).
  • Let it end. Every work listed runs for the week and loops. Ours is the one that will be over.
