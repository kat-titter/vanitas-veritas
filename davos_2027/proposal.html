vanitas_veritas/proposal

proposal 29.09.2026 updated 25.10.2026

veri+as

Working notes for a piece by Kat Titterton: a web basin, live, and a sink, proposed, running the same page. It starts from the Vanitas / Veritas loop, 2026, and asks what happens when the water is allowed to move, and when nothing that goes down the drain is allowed to leave.

The seed of 29 September, updated. What is not built is marked in progress or proposed. The piece: veri+as iii. The context: the black paper.

00  thesis

  • A new biology. New forms of life; the uncanny in-between, of life and death and of species; emergence; softness.
  • you can't wash your hands of it. A sink you cannot wash your hands in. Pictures dreamed by AI drain away and come back from the tap, copies of copies. Generative models now train on their own output; so do markets; so do feeds.
  • sink == sync. Two clocks on one beam fall into step (Huygens); a crowd of clocks does once the coupling passes a threshold (Kuramoto). The glass is a crowd of clocks. When enough people hold still at once, the rhythm breaks and the title turns from vanitas to veritas.
  • we. The answer to the riddle, and the credit line: macrofluidic × we.
  • birth another. Every black hole births a new universe, in Lee Smolin's fecund universes. At the centre of every youniverse is a black hole.
a new biologywe need a new biology. The sink is a closed loop: what goes down the drain comes back out of the tap. What keeps coming back grows new forms of life, each from two of the loop's pictures: a face with the skull showing through, a skull in flower, bubbles becoming cells. Between life and death, between species; emergent, soft. merge.
resistanceresistance is futile. Scrub the glass and it only spreads; the hand comes away gold. you can't wash your hands of it. Wipe the culture on the rim and it divides and grows back. it's all ok.
okit shall be ok. Hold still and the glass clears. Seven stars hide under it; each one found grows a new form of life and writes a line of the program. beautiful even. great even.
the spotlightyou can't hide. The black hole at the centre lights as the sky fills. the spotlight.
the skyEvery find lights a star. Seven make a youniverse, named in Latin as code (spuma_exspectans_concubii: the foam that waits, at first sleep), with the culture your hands pushed. The end reads you back: a line of poem over a line of code that counts what you did. Two letters finish the credit line and open the black paper.

01  the piece

open it full size -> earlier: i

i (29 September) and ii (5 October) are kept as they were. One clock carries into iii: it runs from 29 September to 18 January 2027, and each day a little less is poured and the clocks in the glass are coupled tighter (?day=N looks ahead).

A basin seen from above, under fluted glass, ringed by a living culture. It is born from nothing: a flash, a ring of gold, the sphere, the culture growing out of the rim, the title arriving as v[an]i+as and turning once. Nothing explains itself: a star glints, a tap ripples, and the first hold finds its place under the hand. Seven stars hide under the glass. Each grows a new form of life from two of the loop's 32 pictures, named like a species (Calvaria florens), and adds a line of poem over a line of code. How you play pushes the culture, so each youniverse ends with its own. What drains comes back from the tap ten seconds later, copies of copies. The pulse is 38 bpm, a freediver's heart on a breath-hold.

01b  built, in progress, proposed

built

  • The program. A primer, then a poem, written as Python: a human comment over the machine's code (03).
  • Two poems. The hint poem: one line per find, written from what grew (its join, its two pictures, its place in the seven). Kat's seven lines appear only now and then, later finds lean toward we, and return youniverse(me + you) shows from the start, so anyone who reads code can solve the riddle at once. The reading, at the end: pairs of a kind, slightly obscure line of poem over a line of code that is a literal count of the play (while held < 14.3: stay()). On some days, one line is the same for everyone who played that day. A light play reads short, a deep one long, from three pairs to ten. What chooses the lines stays hidden. The reading sits left-aligned like code, big to small, the name once, symbols for sending.
  • Holding together. Every hold, anywhere, is a ring in everyone's basin, carried over public Nostr relays: no server, no accounts. Every day at noon, central European time, everyone holds. The coupling tightens toward January: in October one long breath can turn the title alone; by January it takes more than one.
  • The study. Each play leaves one anonymous record on public Nostr relays (NIP-78): counts of the play, the shape of its stars, the forms of life by name, the culture and, at the end, the youniverse's name and code. No picture. ?nostudy or Global Privacy Control keeps a play out; there is no opt-out button, by design.
  • Keeping and sending. A youniverse travels as a 52-character link, never a picture. It goes out by symbol (X, Bluesky, Threads, LinkedIn), or with its card through the phone's own share sheet, or saved, or copied; the raw link is never shown. Every youniverse grown stays on the device. birth another starts a new one.
  • The macroverse, in 3D. Every finished youniverse, from the public record and this device, placed by how alike it was played; clusters and filaments form after the fact, and the whole turns slowly. Near, a youniverse is its flat constellation; opened, it folds into its sky with its culture round it, never both at once. home frames your own; the weverse lights you and your friends.

in progress

  • Retests. The macroverse making yours unmistakable; a 0.2 s hitch when a hand first touches the culture; a real phone, by touch.

proposed

  • The black hole grows. The drain grows into a black hole as you find stars, of a kind set by how you played: still, or spinning; rarer, a quasar, a torn star, two merging when you held with someone, a pulsar or a supernova; very rarely, a white hole.
  • birth another, as rebirth. The old youniverse falls into its black hole and a new one is born from it, inheriting its culture with a small mutation. Lineages in the macroverse.
  • The sink. A first sink in San Francisco, running the same page, redesigned (the sink, 04b).

02  what style makes sense

The lenticular. The ridged glass in the singulari+i image and the ridged plastic on a 2000s holo trading card are the same optical object. One is a lens you look through; the other is a collectible you tilt. A lenticular shows two pictures from one surface depending on where you stand, which is already the whole premise of vanitas_veritas.

surfacea fluted lens with holo foil in the highlights; grain
colourink, bone and gold, with ice; red at the black hole's edge, in the farthest stars and in a starved culture
languagea poem that is also the program
objectthe sink, the screen in the drain; seven stars and the black hole; a youniverse kept as a card
timeslow, looping, held breath; every day at noon, everyone holds

September's rules: signs are drawn with one stroke weight, like pictograms on a 2003 device. Fluid is cel-shaded with an inked contour and one hard highlight, so it reads as cartoon, then given real grain and a soft second highlight so it does not quite. No periodic waves. Colour arrives only in a pour or in the foil.

Third pass, after "weird, mysterious, apocalyptic, shamanistic": the sink becomes a scrying basin. Same lens, same signs, but staged as a rite. The disc is a black sun with a fibrous corona that brightens on the beat. Ash rises. Ember and ice are the only colours, one pour at a time. Fog crosses the rim and the glass is scratched. Space is the other material: the disc is cropped by the edge of the screen and the words sit small in what is left.

Where it went. The black sun became the black hole at the centre. Ash, fog and the ring of cuts were dropped. Colour now also lives in the stars and in the culture's threads.

atwo basins

Proposed: two basins, one loop. What drains from one pours from the other's tap, and back, side by side or in two cities. The holds already travel between basins over public relays; a second sink would join them.

Two parties who cannot wash their hands without the other receiving it.

bthe cards

Proposed: lenticular cards, vanitas at one tilt and veritas at the other. Built: any form of life, and every youniverse, can be kept as a card and sent.

cliquidity pool

The basin taken literally. Each visitor adds one drop of pigment. It circulates, dilutes, and never leaves. By Friday the pool is the colour of everyone who came. The colour on the last day is the work.

Liquidity, trickle-down, and who gets to wash their hands.

dnull address

The null address is where tokens are sent to be burned. A drain makes the same promise. Here the promise is broken: whatever is burned into the drain comes back out of the tap.

Mechanic: burn to pour.

ehold

Built, in another form: every hold, anywhere, is a ring in everyone's basin. When enough hold at once the rhythm breaks and the title turns from vanitas to veritas. Two handles on one sink, too far apart for one person, is still proposed.

fsnowmelt

No screen. Real water, a small pump, a fluted acrylic disc over a lit basin, pigment dosed on the heartbeat. The water is snow collected outside the venue on day one and returned on day five.

Hardest to build, easiest to explain.

05  build notes

What the page is, technically. One HTML page, no framework, no server of its own, no accounts. WebGL2. One small library, nostr-tools, carries the shared hold and the study over public relays.

  • Basin: a stable-fluids solver (semi-Lagrangian advection, 36 pressure passes, 24 on phones) on a 256² grid with a source at the tap and a sink at the drain, a swirl that reverses each still, and a 1280² dye field (768² on phones). The dye is the picture: it is fed from the current still and carried by the flow.
  • The return: what is going down the drain is remembered for ten seconds and poured back at the tap, dimmer each pass: copies of copies.
  • Glass: 72 flutes per basin unit. The glass is 2,048 coupled clocks (Kuramoto). In step, bands of light run down the flutes and sink == sync is True. A hold takes tiles out of step, and when enough hold at once the rhythm breaks, the basin clears and the title turns. Each day the coupling is a little stronger.
  • Stills: the 32 anchors the loop was dreamed through (Kat's eight pictures, lightly dreamed, and three dreamed between each pair), 1024² WebP, inlined by build.py. The page walks among them at random. Each find grows a named form of life from two of them, joined as haunt (the living and the dead), graft (across species), culture (grown from the culture's cells) or merge (two as one), in amounts set by how the hand arrived.
  • Culture: Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion on a 4096×256 polar field, drawn as contour threads with glints. It starts at f .031, k .060, where stripes, spots and holes meet. How you play pushes the two numbers: stillness toward a net, moving toward loops, scrubbing toward foam, wiping toward dividing cells, holding with others toward a lattice. Its threads turn red when underfed, gold in the middle, white when fed. Each youniverse ends with its own culture.
  • The youniverse: seven finds make one. A 52-character link (?sky=) carries its stars and each form of life's recipe, never a picture. Whoever opens it finds it under theirs in gold and can grow its forms of life again.
  • Ring of cuts (i only): 240 cuts, long for 1 and short for 0, spelling sink=[sync]*n;n->inf;sink=sync.
  • Finish: a soft-focus pass (quarter-resolution blur, lighter inside the basin), desaturation to 58%, one warm tint, grain. Colours: ink, bone, gold, ice; red at the black hole's edge, in the farthest stars and in a starved culture.
  • Render cost (measured on i): 3 to 9 ms a frame on an M-series laptop at 1.5×2K. Phones get a lighter basin, and without WebGL the page shows one still and the words. Press r to record a 15-second clip.

Toward the installation.

  • The sink, redesigned and not yet built (the sink), seals fluted glass over the basin screen. The glass gets the lens look optically, with no shader; the on-screen lens in the web basin is a stand-in for it.
  • Built in the page: a sink mode (?sink), the disc only, Space as the faucet; it keeps nothing and fills again 75 seconds after each ending. Proposed for the sink: a sensor on the lever that types Space.

06  where we stand

  • The shortest ritual in the room. One breath. It fits between anything, and what you grew leaves in the pocket, as a link.
  • Put it where they wash their hands. The sink belongs in the room people enter alone.
  • Live data, but ours is people holding still. Every hold, anywhere, reaches every basin.
  • Faces: our own, not borrowed. The faces melting in the basin are the artist's.
  • Duration as material. Every day at noon, everyone holds: on the web now, and at the sink (proposed).
  • Let it end. Most installations run for the show and loop. Ours is the one that will be over.
