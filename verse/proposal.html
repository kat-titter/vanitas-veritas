vanitas_veritas/proposal

proposal 29.09.2026 updated 25.10.2026

vanitas_veritas

Working notes for a piece by Kat Titterton: a sink you can't wash your hands in, in two places with one water. vanitas, the sink, is a stone basin in a room, proposed; veritas is the same basin on the web, built. It starts from the Vanitas / Veritas loop, 2026, and asks what happens when the loop is opened: when the water is a machine dreaming, and nothing it dreams comes back.

The seed of 29 September, updated. What is not built is marked in progress or proposed. The work: veritas. The context: the black paper.

00  thesis

  • no water, no dream. Machines that dream drink water: one estimate puts the training of a single large language model at 700,000 litres of fresh water1. Here the metaphor is plumbing. The water in the basin is a GPU dreaming; switch it off and the tap closes.
  • why a sink. Slop is everything but the kitchen sink. Under tiat's gallery the basement keeps “a slop station (an empty room with one metal sink)”2. On 26 October 2022 Elon Musk carried a sink into Twitter's headquarters, “let that sink in”3.
  • you can't wash your hands of it. A sink you cannot wash your hands in. Reach in and the dream goes where your hand goes, then slips through your fingers, down the drain.
  • sink == sync. Two clocks on one beam fall into step (Huygens)4. Two basins hang from one spring: a hand in either moves the water in both. vanitas and veritas are one swap of letters apart, as sink and sync are one sound apart: one water.

01  the piece

open it full size ->

On the published site the tap is dry until the spring has a lasting address: water = None. While the spring runs on Kat's Mac, the frames pour there and, through a tunnel, for anyone with its address.

A basin seen from above, a 4:3 rectangle with rounded corners like the frames it carries, under ribbed glass, with a tap at the top and a drain in the middle. The spring writes a new prompt in the grammar of Kat's Midjourney recipe, vanitas [flower, skull, soap bubble], never the same words twice for 1.3 million prompts, about five years; dreams it out of the last dream, pulled back toward her vanitas moodboard; and dreams three more places on the way to the next, gliding through them, a frame every two to three seconds, each 2560 by 1920. The frames pour from the tap to the drain, and besides its name, the only words are code: the prompt, and the grip while you hold. Press, and the water under your hand moves with it, then slips through your fingers. The space bar is the plug. When the spring stops, the tap closes and the basin drains.

01b  built, in progress, proposed

built

  • The spring. code/sink.py: Stable Diffusion 1.5 with LCM-LoRA56, graded with the palette lock the sources were graded with. A dream takes about four seconds on an Apple M5; the chip's Neural Engine enlarges every frame four times with Real-ESRGAN while the GPU dreams on. It keeps its last sixty frames and how far through its prompts it has dreamed.
  • veritas. One page that pours whatever the spring dreamed last, through a stable-fluids solver7, and closes the tap when nothing new has come for twenty seconds.
  • One water. A touch in any basin travels as an anonymous, ephemeral event over public Nostr relays and moves the water in every other basin: a push, a grip, a thin ring of light; gold if the hand is on vanitas. Nothing is stored.
  • vanitas, in software. veritas in its vanitas mode: touching the water never opens the camera; holding its own eye on the glass for two seconds does, for under a second, and sends one frame of a face to the spring, with a key, as the reference for the next dream, the open-model version of Midjourney's omni reference. The line of code then ends --oref you. The frame is used once in memory and never written down. The space bar is the lever: down, generation stops, no data flows, and every basin drains; up, it floods back.

in progress

  • A lasting address for the spring. Today veritas is public through a tunnel from Kat's Mac whenever the spring runs; the site shows iii until the address lasts.
  • The spring's taste. Its prompts and grade tuned against her vanitas moodboard, dream by dream.

proposed

  • vanitas. A first sink in San Francisco: a basalt column with the basin as its screen, under ribbed glass that answers a touch; a small camera where a tap would stand; a lever that is the tap; a QR code cut into its top that opens veritas (its page, 04b).
  • The GPU in the plinth. vanitas becomes the spring, the lever its own switch, and veritas, wherever it is open, drinks from a room in San Francisco.
  • A second sink. Another city, the same water.

02  what style makes sense

The lenticular. Ridged glass over a picture and the ridged plastic on a 2000s holo trading card are the same optical object. One is a lens you look through; the other is a collectible you tilt. A lenticular shows two pictures from one surface depending on where you stand, which is already the whole premise of vanitas_veritas.

surfaceribbed glass over the water; grain
colourink, bone and gold, with ice; the dreams graded silver and amber like the sources
languageone line of code: the prompt being dreamed
objectvanitas, a stone basin whose screen is the water
timenever the same twice; a frame every two to three seconds, until the tap closes

September's rules: signs are drawn with one stroke weight, like pictograms on a 2003 device. Fluid is cel-shaded with an inked contour and one hard highlight, so it reads as cartoon, then given real grain and a soft second highlight so it does not quite. No periodic waves. Colour arrives only in a pour or in the foil.

Third pass, after "weird, mysterious, apocalyptic, shamanistic": the drawn sink becomes a scrying basin. Same lens, same signs, but staged as a rite. The disc is a black sun with a fibrous corona that brightens on the beat. Ash rises. Ember and ice are the only colours, one pour at a time. Fog crosses the rim and the glass is scratched. Space is the other material: the disc is cropped by the edge of the screen and the words sit small in what is left.

Where it went. The black sun became the drain. Ash, fog and the ring of cuts were dropped. The disc became a rectangle, 4:3 like the dreams, and the colour now comes only from what the spring dreams.

04  six ways in

atwo basins

Proposed: two sinks, one water, side by side or in two cities. Hands already travel between basins over public relays; a second sink would join them.

Two parties who cannot wash their hands without the other feeling it.

bthe cards

Lenticular cards, the word vanitas at one tilt and veritas at the other.

In September, eight signs issued as a set.

cliquidity pool

The basin taken literally. Each visitor adds one drop of pigment. It circulates, dilutes, and never leaves. By the end the pool is the colour of everyone who came. The colour on the last day is the work.

Liquidity, trickle-down, and who gets to wash their hands.

dnull address

The null address is where tokens are sent to be burned. A drain makes the same promise. Here the promise is broken: whatever is burned into the drain comes back out of the tap.

Mechanic: burn to pour.

ehold

Built, in another form: a hand in any basin moves the water in every other. Two handles on one sink, too far apart for one person, stays an idea.

fsnowmelt

No screen. Real water, a small pump, a fluted acrylic disc over a lit basin, pigment dosed on the heartbeat. The water is snow collected outside the venue on the first day and returned on the last.

Hardest to build, easiest to explain.

In September the recommendation was a + b. Where it went: e is built, as one water between basins. A second sink (a) is proposed for after the first. b, c, d and f stay ideas.

05  build notes

What the page is, technically. veritas is one HTML page, no framework, no build step, no accounts. WebGL2. One small library, nostr-tools, carries the hands between basins over public relays.

  • Basin: a stable-fluids solver (semi-Lagrangian advection, 36 pressure passes, 24 on phones) on a 256² grid, in a 4:3 rectangle with rounded corners, with a source at the tap and a vortex at the drain, and a 1024² dye field (768² on phones, up to 2048² on large screens). The dye is the picture: the frames pour in as the pigment, re-forming out of the newest frame, freshest where the tap lands.
  • The hand: a grip pulls the water under the palm toward the hand's own motion, except in five gaps where the fingers part, and loosens by the second (grip = 0.55 after one). Under the hand the glass lies flat and the moment you reached in stops changing.
  • The plug: the space bar closes the drain and the dream pools; when it opens, everything goes.
  • The tap: veritas asks the spring for its newest frame every second or so. When nothing new has come for twenty seconds, the tap closes, what is left runs to the drain, and the line reads water = None. Without WebGL2 the page shows an empty basin.
  • The spring: code/sink.py, Stable Diffusion 1.5 with LCM-LoRA at 640×480, ten steps, guidance 1.5, ten walk frames between two dreams. Frames land in spring_feed/, outside git, beside one small file that says what is newest.
  • One water: ephemeral Nostr events (kind 20777, tag veritas-basin) over three public relays, carrying where a hand is, whether it grips, and whether it is on vanitas. ?solo keeps a basin to itself.
  • The face (vanitas only): with ?spring=…&sink&camera&key=…, holding the camera's eye for two seconds takes one frame, at most once every twenty seconds; touching the water never does. The spring finds the largest face, crops it 4:3, grades it like the sources and dreams from it once. It is never written to disk.

Toward the installation.

  • vanitas, proposed and not yet built (its page), seals ribbed glass over a touch screen. The glass gets the lens look optically, with no shader.
  • Built in the page: the vanitas mode, the basin edge to edge, the space bar as the lever. Proposed for vanitas: a sensor on the lever that holds the space bar while it is down.

06  where we stand

  • The shortest ritual in the room. One reach. It fits between anything, and nothing leaves with you.
  • Put it where they wash their hands. A sink you cannot wash your hands in says the rest without a word.
  • Live data, but ours is a machine dreaming. And every hand, anywhere, reaches every basin.
  • Faces: given, not borrowed. The dreams lean toward the artist's own frames; in vanitas, a face is given by holding the camera's eye, dreamed once, and let go.
  • Duration as material. 1.3 million prompts, about five years, before the words come round again.
  • Let it stop. Most installations run for the show and loop. This one stops when the GPU does, and anyone at vanitas can stop it.
